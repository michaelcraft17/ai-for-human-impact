About the challenge

AI is changing how we learn, work, and connect — but many of the challenges that matter most to our communities still lack accessible, human-centered solutions. This hackathon challenges BASIS students to start with a real problem, not a technology looking for a use case.

"What problem matters to the people I'm building for, and how can AI help solve it?"

Impact-first

Start with a real problem, not a technology looking for a use case.

Designed with people

Projects should involve the community they aim to serve — in research, design, or testing.

AI with a purpose

AI should play a meaningful role. A chatbot wrapper alone won't cut it.

Four tracks

Pick the track closest to your team's passion — or propose your own idea in the Open Category.

AI + Health

健康

Tools that help people understand, manage, or access healthcare and improve their wellbeing.

EXAMPLES caregiver organization tools, clearer medical information, healthy-routine assistants

AI + Education

教育

Tools that help students learn, teachers teach, and education become more personalized and accessible.

EXAMPLES adaptive tutoring, interactive concept visualizations, early struggle detection for teachers

AI + Accessibility & Inclusion

包容与无障碍

Tools that empower people with disabilities, neurodivergent individuals, and communities facing barriers.

EXAMPLES communication support, inclusive navigation, tools for neurodivergent students

AI + Sustainability & Community

环境与社会

Solutions for healthier communities, environmental protection, and social challenges.

EXAMPLES food-waste reduction, disaster-response coordination, community resource allocation

Eligibility & requirements

The short version — full rules are posted on Devpost.

  • Open to high school students (grades 9–12) at any BASIS school nationwide
  • Compete solo or in teams of up to 5
  • Minors need parent/guardian permission to participate
  • AI must be used meaningfully — not just a chatbot wrapper
  • Every project must involve at least one real user in design or testing
  • Submit a demo video, project write-up, and public GitHub repo

Frequently asked

Do I need advanced AI experience?

No. This is beginner-friendly — workshops, mentors, and community support will help you turn an idea into a working prototype, no matter your starting point.

Can I compete solo?

Yes. You can enter alone or in a team of up to 5 people. Team members don't need to attend the same BASIS campus.

What if my idea doesn't fit one of the four tracks?

Enter the Open Category. Just clearly explain the problem it addresses, who benefits, and why AI is an appropriate part of the solution.

Is this open to every BASIS campus?

Yes — this hackathon is open to high school students at any BASIS school nationwide.
