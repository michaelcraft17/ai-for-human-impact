About the challenge

What is AI for Human Impact?

AI for Human Impact is a hackathon for BASIS high school students. Over one week, teams use AI to build real solutions to real problems — for people, not just for a demo.

What you'll build

Teams design and build an AI-powered project addressing a genuine need in health, education, accessibility, or sustainability — shaped by the people it's meant to help, not built in a vacuum.

What you'll get

Workshops, mentors, and community support to help you go from idea to working prototype — plus $10,000+ in prizes and recognition across four tracks.

Eligibility & requirements

The short version — full rules are posted on Devpost.

Who's Eligible

  • Open to high school students (grades 9–12) at any BASIS school nationwide
  • Compete solo or in teams of up to 5
  • Minors need parent/guardian permission to participate

What's Required

  • AI must be used meaningfully — not just a chatbot wrapper
  • Every project must involve at least one real user in design or testing
  • Submit a demo video, project write-up, and public GitHub repo

Frequently asked

Do I need advanced AI experience?

No. This is beginner-friendly — workshops, mentors, and community support will help you turn an idea into a working prototype, no matter your starting point.

Can I compete solo?

Yes. You can enter alone or in a team of up to 5 people. Team members don't need to attend the same BASIS campus.

What if my idea doesn't fit one of the four tracks?

Enter the Open Category. Just clearly explain the problem it addresses, who benefits, and why AI is an appropriate part of the solution.

Is this open to every BASIS campus?

Yes — this hackathon is open to high school students at any BASIS school nationwide.
